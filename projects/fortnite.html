Fly Studio internship

Fortnite × Times Square Digital Experience

A custom Fortnite Creative map tied to the Times Square New Year's Eve celebration, connecting online players with a real-world global event.

Overview

During my internship at Fly Studio, I helped design and launch a Fortnite Creative map for a New Year's Eve campaign with One Times Square. The experience was promoted through Fortnite's official Instagram and drew hundreds of players into the Times Square celebration event.

My role

  • Contributed to the planning and design of the interactive Fortnite Creative map
  • Worked with the broader team to align the experience with event timing and audience engagement
  • Supported the rollout and visibility of the experience across digital channels

Outcome

The campaign reached 2M+ views on Fortnite's official Instagram and showed how digital interactive spaces can amplify real-world events. It strengthened my interest in experiential digital products and large-scale audience engagement.
